Purchase Order Approval

A demo of integration & automation on SAP BTP: creating a purchase order automatically triggers an approval workflow in SAP Integration Suite (CPI) and manages the status end-to-end in a CAP service, all through a Fiori Elements UI.

Open the Fiori app
  • •  OData service: /odata/v4/purchase-order
  • •  Source code: github.com/koatedevopskpai/sap-po-approval-demo

Create a PO in the app and watch it auto-submit for approval, then Approve or Reject it.
